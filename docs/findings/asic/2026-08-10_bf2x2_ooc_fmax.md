## F15. The block's limiting structure is the Barrett path, and only the failing points reveal it

**EVIDENCE WITHDRAWN 2026-08-12.** Measured with four modules blackboxed. See banner.

The Barrett logic was never synthesized. `barrett_datai` is the register feeding a blackbox boundary.

Every VIOLATED point (C1 to C4) binds on a `barrett_datai` endpoint and its
`Path 1` contains `mul_170` and `barrett` instances. Every MET point (C5 to
C8) binds elsewhere and contains neither. The split is exact across eight
points and four distinct periods on each side.

**Verification:** endpoint and grep counts read from the eight committed
timing reports, tabulated above.

**Mechanism.** Genus stops optimizing when the constraint is satisfied, so a
MET point's `Path 1` shows where the tool happened to stop, not where the
design is limited. Only when the target is unreachable does the tool push
every path and expose the true limiter.

**Implication for the method.** Endpoint identity from a MET run carries no
information about the design. This generalizes the caveat already attached to
A1 in the 08-08 chip doc and to F-B in the handoff, and it is the reason a
bracket search is necessary rather than a single constrained run. It also
means the four MET endpoints in this sweep, `ajlen5`, `ajlen2_INTT`, `ajlen5`,
`aj4`, should not be read as four different findings about the design.

## F16. The adder appears only at the loosest constraint, and is a ripple-carry chain

**EVIDENCE WITHDRAWN 2026-08-12.** Measured with four modules blackboxed. See banner.

`add_169_28` appears on `Path 1` at 600 ps only (C8), as twelve cells in a
contiguous chain, cumulative arrival 201 ps to 505 ps, so roughly 304 ps of a
600 ps period. The cell sequence is `NOR2`, `OA21`, `OA21`, `OAI21`, `AO21`,
`AOI21`, `OAI21`, `AO21`, `AO21`, `AO21`, `AO21`, terminating in `XOR2x2`.
That is a ripple-carry structure.

**Verification:** `logs/asic/asap7_bf2x2_fmax_20260810/butterfly2x2_p600_timing.rpt`
lines 44 to 58. Identified by instance name, per the rule in F-D of the
handoff, not by cell type.

**Implication.** This is the chip's ripple-carry adder finding reproduced at
block level, and it confirms the third clause of the claim "Genus produced a
ripple-carry adder for this RTL, this library, and this timing constraint."
But it appears at exactly one of eight points, the loosest, and vanishes as
soon as the constraint tightens. The adder is not the block's limiter. Any
paper text asserting the adder as a target must state the constraint at which
that is true.

## F17. 578 ps and 581 ps are not distinguishable, shown by an area inversion

C6 at 581 ps reports larger area than C5 at 578 ps (55503.838 against
54896.407) and higher power (9.833 against 9.757 mW), despite the looser
constraint. A looser constraint producing a larger implementation is a tool
nondeterminism signature, not a design property.

**Verification:** both rows read from committed reports of the same arm under
identical configuration, differing only in requested period.

**Implication.** The search's 5 ps TOL is finer than the flow's own
reproducibility at this scale. Report the closure as approximately 578 ps and
do not treat 3 ps differences as signal. This is a second, independent
demonstration of the point made by F3 at 10.9 percent for effort setting: TOL
resolution is not the same thing as measurement resolution. Any future A/B at
block level needs a delta larger than this floor, and the floor should be
measured, not assumed.

any anomaly in the sweeps, which forces the reinterpretation in F17S.

## F17S. Supersedes F17. The 578/581 inversion is deterministic, not noise

F17 read the C5/C6 area and power inversion as a tool nondeterminism
signature and concluded the flow's run-to-run variation exceeds 3 ps. F22
shows there is no run-to-run variation: E5 and E6 reproduce C5 and C6 exactly.

The inversion is real and repeatable. At 581 ps, a looser constraint than
578 ps, Genus produces a larger and hotter implementation, every time. The
same pattern appears on the optimized arm, where D4 at 569 ps draws less power
than D5 at 573 ps and D6 at 577 ps despite being the tightest of the three,
and where cell count rises monotonically as the period loosens across 569,
573 and 577.

**Mechanism, stated as a hypothesis rather than a finding.** Genus stops when
the constraint is satisfied. Small changes to the target can put it on a
different optimization trajectory that terminates at a different, and
sometimes worse, implementation. The response to the constraint is
nonmonotonic in a neighbourhood, not noisy.

**What changes for the paper.** The claim "the flow has a 3 ps
reproducibility floor" is withdrawn; it would have undercut every number in
both sweeps and it is false. What replaces it is narrower: PPA at a given
period is exact and repeatable, but PPA is not monotonic in period over small
intervals, so two nearby periods should not be differenced to infer a trend.
Report measured points, not interpolations between them.

**What does not change.** The caution against quoting a derived frequency to
five significant figures stands, for the different reason that the search
resolution is 5 ps and the closure period is the measurement.

## F23. The optimized arm meets a period the baseline cannot

**EVIDENCE WITHDRAWN 2026-08-12.** Measured with four modules blackboxed. See banner.

At 573 ps, identical configuration, the baseline violates by 6 ps and the
optimized arm meets. Both reproduced (E1/E2, E3/E4).

| | Baseline (E1, E2) | Optimized (E3, E4) |
|---|---|---|
| Result | VIOLATED -6 ps | MET 0 ps |
| Path 1 endpoint | `barrett_datai_reg[34]` | `ajlen5_reg[23]` |
| Sequential | 2361 | 3029 |
| Cells | 19063 | 19327 |
| Total area | 56207.194 | 59183.427 |
| Total power | 9.956 mW | 10.507 mW |

Deltas: area +5.3 percent, power +5.5 percent, cells +1.4 percent, flops +668.

**Verification:** four committed runs, two per arm, every field identical
within each arm.

**How to state this.** The defensible claim is categorical, not numerical: the
optimized arm closes at 573 ps where the baseline does not, at the stated PPA
cost. The 6 ps margin should not be quoted as a magnitude, both because the
baseline's own closure is 578 ps at 5 ps search resolution and because F17S
shows nearby periods are not safely differenced. The C-close and D-close
periods, 578 ps and 573 ps, differ by 5 ps, exactly TOL, which is why the
same-period head-to-head was run instead of comparing the two closures.

## F24. The added pipeline stage moved the limiter, it did not shorten a path

**EVIDENCE WITHDRAWN 2026-08-12.** Measured with four modules blackboxed. See banner.

Every VIOLATED point on the baseline binds `barrett_datai_reg` (C1 to C4,
E1, E2). Every VIOLATED point on the optimized arm binds `mult_p_reg` (D1 to
D4). Those are different registers, and the difference is exactly the added
stage: the baseline has `barrett_datai <= mult_result` while the optimized arm
has `mult_p <= mult_result` followed by `barrett_datai <= mult_p`.

**Verification:** endpoints from eight committed timing reports across the two
sweeps, plus E1 and E2.

**Implication.** The optimized arm's advantage is not a faster multiplier. It
is a shorter segment, because the multiplier-to-Barrett path was split by a
register. That is worth stating precisely in the paper, since "optimized arm
is faster" invites the reader to assume logic-level optimization when the
mechanism is pipelining, which costs 668 flops and shows up directly in the
area and power deltas of F23.

## F25. F15 reproduces on a second arm

**EVIDENCE WITHDRAWN 2026-08-12.** Measured with four modules blackboxed. See banner.

F15 established on the baseline that only VIOLATED points reveal the limiting
structure, since a MET point's `Path 1` shows where the tool stopped. The
optimized sweep reproduces the split exactly: all four VIOLATED points bind
`mult_p_reg` and carry `mul` instances on `Path 1`; all four MET points bind
`ajlen5_reg` and carry none.

**Verification:** grep counts and endpoints from the eight D-series reports.

**Implication.** The rule is not an artifact of one arm. Combined with F22,
it is now the strongest methodological claim available from this work: a
single MET run, which is what the 78-hour chip run A1 is, carries no
information about where the design is limited. The chip doc should carry a
pointer to F15 and F25 together.

## F26. The optimized arm shows the same one-point adder appearance

**EVIDENCE WITHDRAWN 2026-08-12.** Measured with four modules blackboxed. See banner.

`add_174` appears on `Path 1` at 600 ps only (D8), as 14 cells. The baseline
shows `add_169` at 600 ps only (C8), as 12 cells. Same behaviour, same
period, both arms, different operator names.

**Implication.** F16's caveat carries over unchanged: the adder is not the
block's limiter on either arm, and any paper text targeting it must state the
constraint at which it is critical.
